# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in arxiv2zh a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include the repository,
Issues, pull requests, release discussions, and other official communication.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
`caihua_sau@163.com`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to
the extent practical. Do not include unrelated private data in a report.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, code, Issues, or other
contributions that violate this Code. Depending on severity and recurrence,
responses may include a private correction, a warning, temporary suspension, or
a permanent ban from project spaces.
